I desire to understand things deeply, to clarify, to know the truth that will set me free. Understanding seems more fitting than discovery. It applies to the personal as well as the impersonal, to my humanistic interests as well as my scientific interests.

But what things would God have me understand? And how will I find opportunity to share what I understand?

What do I think I know? Write it down. This writing is teaching.

What are my open questions? Write them down. This writing is journaling.

I read, learn, research, calculate, and synthesize so that I can write, teach, discuss, and share.
