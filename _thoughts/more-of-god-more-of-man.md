When I ask for "more of God," perhaps His answer is "more of man."

A human answer to my prayer would not be "less God" (Barth).

Perhaps God would like to bless me, or be closer to me, through his people, through my interactions with those I care for and those who love me. His church, of which I am a part; those I love and serve, and even my enemies.
