It is not that I feel unloved. I feel loved by God and family. But there is a burden that I am carrying, and I am carrying it alone.

When I say "carry it alone," I do not mean that the burden is for those I love. Not at all. I want to release my own load so that I can love them and lighten theirs.

Perhaps the weight is imagined and Jesus invites me into the truth that his burden is light. Or perhaps I am in the ridiculous position of hesitating rather than allowing Jesus to wash me.
